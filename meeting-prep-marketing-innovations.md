## 5. The Proposal: A "Marketing Fast Lane" Process

### The Problem (Frame It This Way)
"Right now, marketing has ideas and innovation has capabilities, but there's no structured fast lane connecting them. Requests go through informal channels, approvals are unclear, and things stall. We're proposing a simple, repeatable process that keeps Jess in control while letting Chai ship 5-10x faster."

### The Proposed Process

```
Step 1: BRIEF (30 min)
Chai submits a one-paragraph brief: what she needs, who it's for, when she needs it.

Step 2: SKETCH (2-4 hours)
Antonio + AI produce 3 visual options (HTML sketches, live URLs to review).
No code investment yet — these are disposable prototypes.

Step 3: PICK (15 min async)
Jess and Chai review sketches, pick one direction, leave comments.
This is the quality gate. Nothing proceeds without a pick.

Step 4: BUILD (4-8 hours)
Selected sketch gets polished to production quality.
Brand system ensures consistency automatically.

Step 5: SHIP (30 min)
Deploy to Vercel. Chai gets the live URL. Done.

Total: 1-2 days from brief to live, vs. 2-6 weeks traditionally.
```

### Why Jess Should Love This
1. **She sees everything before it ships.** The sketch step is a built-in review gate.
2. **She picks, not approves.** Instead of reviewing one thing and saying yes/no, she picks from options. This feels empowering, not blocking.
3. **Brand is protected.** The design system (colors, typography, aesthetic) is baked into the pipeline. It's not possible to ship something off-brand.
4. **It's reversible.** Landing pages on Vercel can be updated or taken down in minutes. Low risk.
5. **It costs nothing extra.** No new hires, no agency fees, no software subscriptions beyond what we already have.

### Why Chai Should Love This
1. **She gets deliverables in days, not weeks.**
2. **She sees options, not just one take-it-or-leave-it design.**
3. **She can request iterations without guilt** — changes take minutes, not billable hours.
4. **She can run more campaigns** because the bottleneck (production speed) is removed.
5. **She gets technical assets too** — not just pretty pages, but functional tools (calling, LinkedIn outreach, interactive content).
