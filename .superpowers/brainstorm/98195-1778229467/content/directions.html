The bet

One screen. Smart defaults from last run. Mode is a chip, not a section. Forecast updates live. Operator confirms and launches in <10 seconds.

You'd love this if…

  • You run the same shape of campaign every day and resent re-doing the wizard
  • You can tolerate density in exchange for speed
  • "Power-user dashboard" feels right, not "guided setup"

Trade-off

First-time user gets less hand-holding. Edge-case modes (intro_back, post_amp) live in "More ▾".

The bet

The first decision is "what job am I doing?" — and that decision strips the screen. Connect-only campaigns never see Check Status fields and vice versa.

You'd love this if…

  • You operate in distinct sessions ("connect day" vs "follow-up day")
  • You think the 8 modes are the source of cognitive load, not the controls
  • You want to onboard new operators on one mode at a time

Trade-off

Each lane is fast, but switching lanes mid-thought is a navigation cost. Power users may miss the "everything visible" feel.

The bet

A vertical conversation. One question at a time, top to bottom, no tabs, no sections, no horizontal layout. Each answer summons the next prompt.

You'd love this if…

  • You want the app to feel friendly and modern, not "enterprise admin panel"
  • New operators need to be talked through their first few campaigns
  • You're OK trading some speed for guidance and approachability

Trade-off

Slower for power users (still N steps, just nicer). Less spatial — you can't glance at "everything at once." Forecast lives at the bottom, not always visible.
