The bet

One screen replaces the 5-step wizard. Smart defaults from last run pre-fill everything. Mode is a chip, not a section. Forecast updates live as you toggle. Launch in <10 seconds when nothing's changed.

You'd love this if…

  • You run the same shape of campaign daily and resent re-doing the wizard
  • You can tolerate a denser screen in exchange for speed
  • "Power-user dashboard" beats "guided setup" for your team

Trade-off

First-time users get less hand-holding. Edge modes (Introduce Back, InMail, Post Amp, Check DMs) live in More ▾. The 6-min floor / parallel toggle still need a tooltip explainer.

The bet

The first decision — "what job am I doing?" — strips the screen. Connect campaigns never see Check Status fields. Each lane is its own minimal form, sized to fit on one screen.

You'd love this if…

  • You operate in distinct sessions ("connect day" vs "follow-up day")
  • You think 8 modes upfront is the source of cognitive load — not the controls themselves
  • You want to onboard new operators one mode at a time

Trade-off

Switching lanes mid-thought is a navigation cost. Power users may miss "everything visible at once." The secondary modes (intro back, check DMs…) sit in a quieter pill row below.

The bet

A vertical conversation. One question at a time, each answer summons the next prompt. The forecast lives quietly at the bottom; the controls only appear when relevant. Feels modern, friendly — closer to Linear or Notion than admin panel.

You'd love this if…

  • You want the app to feel friendly and modern, not "enterprise admin panel"
  • New operators need to be talked through their first few campaigns
  • You'd trade some speed for guidance and approachability

Trade-off

Slower for power users — still N steps, just nicer ones. Less spatial: you can't glance at "everything at once." The forecast is at the bottom, not always pinned in view.
