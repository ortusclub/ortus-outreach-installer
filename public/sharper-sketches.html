The whole change, in one sentence

A button that's working looks like it's working — spinner, ticking seconds, and the name of what it's waiting on — and past ~25 seconds it says why it's slow, so a long wait reads as a long wait instead of a crash.

Same treatment on all four places you can press these: the wizard's Start button, the campaign-tab card, the dashboard strip, and the board rows. They already share the same handlers, so it's one change, not four.
