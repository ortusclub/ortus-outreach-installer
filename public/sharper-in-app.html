Why this is a small change

Every piece already exists in the app. .vj-live is the card's “what's happening now” row. vj-live-spin is the spinner used by .vj-live.is-checking. vj-queued-pulse is the gold pulsing dot used by queued campaigns. .dock-btn already has a disabled style.

The new CSS is the six rules at the top of this file's <style> block. The rest is wiring the existing handlers — _doStopCloud, pauseCloudCampaignUI, _submitCloudCampaign — to set and clear one is-pending class, and to keep ticking until the engine confirms rather than assuming the click worked.
