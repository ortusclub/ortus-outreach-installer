1Point it at your tab

Open the tab inside your sheet, then copy the URL from the address bar — it should include #gid=…

This workbook has multiple tabs — choose the invite list
Your tab needs two columns:
LinkedIn URL · Account Email
Anything else in the sheet is left alone. Account Email = the GoLogin login that sends that invite.
