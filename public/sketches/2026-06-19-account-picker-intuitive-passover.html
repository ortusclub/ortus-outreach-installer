Account picker — built for "just tell me which one to use"

The earlier three were too clever — abstract dots, no plain meaning, and nothing about passover. These three lead with a plain-English verdict per account, and every one surfaces passover: monthly credits (OP · InMail · Sales Nav) rest the 1st→15th and refresh on the 16th; weekly Connect (CC) rests Mon→Wed and refreshes Thursday. All three share the banner below.

⏳
Passover — example: today is the 12th.
Monthly credits (OP · InMail · Sales Nav) are resting until the 16th — in 4 days. Weekly Connect (CC) is active until Sunday. Accounts below are tagged so you don't pick one that can't send today.

(The date is an example to show passover in action — the real banner reads the live date. On the 19th, like today, both monthly and CC are active and the banner says so.)

A coloured edge (🟢 go / 🟡 busy or resting / 🔴 stop) and one sentence telling you what to do. No dots to decode. The passover refresh date sits right under the verdict so a resting account explains itself.
