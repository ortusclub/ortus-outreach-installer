Live card · legibility & button labels

Three changes, shown in every state a campaign can be in. 1) the pending-leads pill gets real size, and goes gold whenever leads are still unsent, so "92 pending, sending is stopped" cannot be mistaken for a quiet detail. 2) the fact rows keep their small mono labels but the values on the right move up to reading size. 3) the two actions that matter get words instead of glyphs: Open campaign tab, Resume sending, Stop campaign. Restart, duplicate, check-now and show-live stay as icons in the dock.
