Three layouts for the Follower Growth launch screen. The problem today: setup (roles + team browse + launch list) and the live-run card are separate full-width blocks stacked vertically, so when a run starts the live card collides with the sticky launch panel above it. Each variant keeps one launch/monitor surface so nothing overlaps. Toggle below.

Variant 1 — Cockpit

Setup on the left, a single sticky launch panel on the right that becomes the live monitor in place. No second card appears — the right panel just swaps its contents from "launch list" to "live log + progress" when you hit launch. The two never coexist, so there's nothing to overlap.
