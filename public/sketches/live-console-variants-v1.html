Live Console — 4 size / layout sketches

You said the console is "way too small" and you want it to read like the cockpit / log-status section. Here are four directions at realistic scale. The dark panels sit on a light page on purpose, so you can also judge the × close button contrast (it's now bright + larger in all four).

Every field shown is a real field the console already has: campaign name + mode, sent processed / total, current lead / account / action, the selected accounts roster (active one highlighted), and the last few activity log lines. Sample values are illustrative only — no invented stats.

A

Wide drawer

The same right-edge slide-out you have now, but ~40% wider, with bigger fonts, a tall scrollable activity log, and a bright 30px ×. Smallest change — keeps the click-to-open lip exactly as-is.
