The idea: one card component, driven by state. Sending → green accent + arrow, live line shows who it's contacting + lead counter + % done. Monitoring → blue accent + clock, live line shows "Watching" + big next-check countdown. Checking → blue accent + spinning ↻, live line shows which account is sweeping. Paused → grey, live line explains the pause. The dashboard card and the wizard Live Status render the same component, so they always agree.
