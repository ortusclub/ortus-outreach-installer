Lanes — campaigns by status, left-to-right lifecycle

Four horizontal lanes: NOW (running) → WATCHING (monitoring) → QUEUE (queued + drafts) → DONE (past). Cards in any lane can be selected via the tiny check in the top-right corner. Bulk action bar slides up from the bottom when anything is selected. Visual lifecycle — you literally see campaigns flowing left to right.

FIX 1 — STATUS IS SPATIAL

Each lane IS its status. Monitoring is its own visual column, equal weight to Active. You see the full lifecycle at a glance — no scrolling between sections.

FIX 2 — CARDS WITH CHECKS

Each card has a tiny check top-right. Hovering reveals it strongly; selecting it lights the card gold. Bulk-action bar appears at the bottom of the viewport. Cross-lane selection supported.

TRADE-OFF

Most visually distinct. Best on wide screens (≥1300px). Past gets compressed (only ~3 cards visible, rest behind "more"); if you live in Past, alternatives A/B/C are denser.
