What has to change to make this real

Three things, all small, none cosmetic:
1. handleStartCloud reports step transitions instead of being one opaque await — it already knows every step, it just never says so.
2. cloudLaunchStatus() stops hardcoding logs: [] and carries those steps, so the live log is live during launch.
3. Each step escalates against its own budget, and a throw becomes the Reason block verbatim rather than an alert() that disappears.
