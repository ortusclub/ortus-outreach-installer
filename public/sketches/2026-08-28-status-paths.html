Identical to the healthy card, and every word of it is wrong. In this branch "Last check · complete" and "Operator action · none required" are literals (app.js:27573-27576) — there is no input that can make them say anything else. And checked = monitorCheckAccountsChecked || scope falls back to the full count, so a sweep that checked nothing reports "3 of 3". The card is optimistic by construction: it recovers the failure only later, from a different branch, by regex-matching the words "Check finished with an error" out of the log.
