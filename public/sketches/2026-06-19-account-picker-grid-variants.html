C — Dropdowns made uniform

Right now the three config dropdowns wear three different styles: Primary check timing is borderless text with a tiny chevron, Cadence is an inline "Every [select]" with just a bottom rule, and the column picker is a full bordered box. That's the "not uniform anymore". The fix: one control style for all of them — the bordered box (it already exists as .intro-config-select-wide), so every dropdown reads the same.
