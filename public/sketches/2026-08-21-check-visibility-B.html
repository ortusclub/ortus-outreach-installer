B — the per-account step panel, in every state

One panel, one column per account, divided by the hairline the card already uses. The steps are the ones the code actually walks, so a stall reads as the step that never ticked rather than a spinner that never stops. Every line reads out loud: no field dumps, no counters an operator cannot act on. Those stay in Cloud Logging. Same panel on the VM and on this Mac; only the RUNS ON line differs. Every card below is the real #active-card cloned out of the shipping index.html, with its accounts, its controls and its details panel left exactly as they are.
