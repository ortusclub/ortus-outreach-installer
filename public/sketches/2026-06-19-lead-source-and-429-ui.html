B — 429 / pacing (the skipping + intervals fix)

B1 · Pause between leads — big red disclaimer below the safe range

No hard floor (per your call) — default stays 30–60s. But drop the low end under 30s and a big red warning appears. Shown here triggered at 15s. Type 30+ to clear it.

! Danger — this pace will get accounts throttled
Below 30s between invites floods LinkedIn with requests (HTTP 429) — which causes the mass skipping you've been seeing, and walks accounts toward checkpoints, restrictions, and bans. Safe range is 30–120s. Only go lower if you fully understand the risk.
B2 · Pause-on-throttle — operator toggle

When an account hits a 429 / soft-block, the app can either pause that account (safe) or keep going — your choice, per the change you asked for. Default ON (recommended). Challenges/checkpoints always halt + surface to a human regardless.

Pause an account when LinkedIn throttles it (429)
On: the account stops sending and backs off when throttled, then resumes slower — protects it from restriction. Other accounts keep running.
