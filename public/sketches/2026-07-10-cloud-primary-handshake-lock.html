The hard-lock: your Mac accepts, then it's all VM

A cloud CC + IC / CC + DM campaign whose primary is local-only can't be accepted-as by the VM. So the campaign opens with a hard-locked Phase 0: the VM fires each account's connect-request at the primary, then pauses — and your Mac accepts those invitations as the local primary. Nothing touches a lead until it clears. Then the campaign runs 100% on the VM. Three ways to show that waiting state on the live card — pick one.
