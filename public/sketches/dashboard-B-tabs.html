Tabbed views — one type of campaign at a time

Five horizontal tabs at the top — Active, Monitoring, Queued, Drafts, Past. Click one; the page becomes a clean focused list of just that type. No more scrolling past three sections to find what you want. Bulk-select is the default — every row has a checkbox, "Select all" lives in the toolbar, a strip appears above the list when anything is selected.

FIX 1 — MONITORING IS A TAB

Equal-weight tab between Active and Queued. Includes a "next check in" hint in each card's subtext. The tab itself shows a small badge when there are unseen monitoring events.

FIX 2 — SELECT-FIRST

Every row already has a checkbox. "Select all" is the leftmost item in the toolbar. Bulk-action strip appears above the list — visible, not hidden behind a menu.

TRADE-OFF

Forces a context-switch to see other categories. Best when you spend most of your time in one tab. Search is per-tab — scoped, faster.
