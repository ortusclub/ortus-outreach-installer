Warm-up · entry-point variants

Three ways to arm account warm-up in the account picker. Goal: make it obvious what a warm-up session is, and make it always reversible. Rendered with the real style.css tile/pill classes. Pick a direction — we don't commit yet.

Variant A
Warm-up mode pill (next to “Deselect all”)

A pill sits in the bulk row. Turning it on reveals the explainer + puts a small “Warm up?” toggle on each free tile. Turning it off hides them. + discoverable, one obvious place, easy to explain. — warm-up is per-account, so the pill only reveals per-tile controls (it doesn't warm all).

What is warm-up? A fresh LinkedIn account can't send at full volume without risk. Warm-up ramps it: week 1 → 5/day · week 2 → 10/day · week 3 → 20/day · then normal limit. Turn it on per account below — toggle it off anytime.
Variant B
In-tile toggle (fixes the revert bug head-on)

Keeps warm-up on the tile where the account lives, but as a real on/off toggle that stays visible in EVERY tile state — so it can never get stranded “on”. A “?” opens the explainer inline. + smallest change, directly kills the “can't revert” bug (the Stop control never disappears). — toggle repeats on every tile; more visual noise across a long grid.

? → Warm-up ramps a fresh account: wk1 5/day · wk2 10/day · wk3 20/day · then normal. The cap only ever lowers the campaign's daily limit, for this account only.
Variant C
“New account?” drawer (dedicated panel)

A single pill opens a drawer that lists every account currently warming up, with a one-tap Stop each, plus an “add account to warm-up” picker. Tiles stay clean; warm-up lives in one managed place. + cleanest grid, one roster to review/revert, best for many accounts. — most build; another panel to maintain.
