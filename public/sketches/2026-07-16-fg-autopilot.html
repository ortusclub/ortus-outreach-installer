FG Auto-Pilot

Follower Growth runs itself — the cloud VM fires the whole team's invites on the 1st and the 15th, with no one clicking launch. The batch is rebuilt live each time: whoever's eligible that day is in.
Runs on the Cloud VM · works with the app closed
How a run happens
1
The clock fires — 1st & 15th, 09:00
A timer on the VM wakes up. Not your laptop — this keeps working when everyone's asleep.
2
Safety check
Is a team launch already running? If yes, this cycle is skipped and logged — never two at once.
3
Build the eligible roster — liveoption 2
Every employee who has a paired GoLogin profile, credits left this month, and isn't logged out is pulled in. The list self-adjusts — no fixed roster to maintain.
4
Match & dedupe their connections
Each account's matching connections are gathered and de-duplicated across the team, so two accounts never invite the same person.
5
Dispatch to the VM
Handed off to the same cloud Team-Launch pipeline you use today — one account at a time, capped at each account's monthly budget.
6
Invites go out
Follow-invites fire sequentially. Logged-out or capped accounts are skipped with a reason.
7
Lands on the board
Shows up as the same live card you already know, and writes back to the FG sheet — identical to a manual cloud run.
