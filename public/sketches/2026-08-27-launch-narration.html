Constant updates, and what the waiting line should say

Three things from the recording: the log said LAST 0 EVENTS for the whole launch, the account-wait line does not explain itself, and the card lags reality by up to 10 seconds. Nothing below is built yet.

1 · The launch log

The launch card already exists and already narrates its four steps. What it does not do is write anything to the log, because cloudLaunchStatus() returns a hardcoded empty array. The rule I would apply: every step says what it is about to do BEFORE it blocks, so a slow step is a step you can name.

Every one of those lines wraps an await that already exists in the launch — the sheet read, the pre-flight call, the dispatch POST. No new work, no new requests. And when a step fails, the same line carries the reason instead of the launch dying into an alert box.

2 · The account-wait line

3 · The 10-second lag

Pressing Stop showed "Cloud campaign stopped" while the card kept saying it was sending. The card is not slow to draw, it is slow to ask.

MomentTodayProposed
You press Stop / Pausewait for the next 5s poll repaint immediately from the action's own response
Engine changes state on its ownup to 5s up to 5s, unchanged
While a launch is in flightno log, 4-step card a log line per step, as above
Only the first row changes. An action you just took should never wait for a poll to be believed, and that is one repaint at the end of the existing Stop / Pause handlers, not a faster timer. Polling harder is the wrong fix — it costs an engine request every second for every open card, and the board already saturates its connection budget.
