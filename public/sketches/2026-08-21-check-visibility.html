Making a running check visible

Measured today: the sweep DID run (server log 12:34:17, three accounts, two full diag lines) while the card read "nothing running right now". Two causes. The status poll only runs while running === true, so a monitoring campaign polls once at page load and then freezes. And the sweep's own detail goes to stdout, never into campaign.logs, so the Live Log could not show it however often it polled. Every card below is the real #active-card cloned out of the shipping index.html, with its accounts, its controls and its details panel left exactly as they are. Only the marked component is new.
