VM logging: before and after

Correction to what I said earlier: the VM does have an operator log, and it is written in plain English already. appendMonitorLog() pushes lines into Redis per campaign and the app reads them as monitorLog. Everything on the left is the real feed for MAEN_ANZ_HOB - CCII+CCIII+CCIV, read off the running VM at 13:15 today, unedited. So this is not "the VM logs nothing". It is: the sweep is well covered, sending is nearly silent, and there are five-minute holes where the feed says nothing at all while the VM is working. The right-hand pane keeps the exact same voice and fills the holes. No counters, no field dumps, no jargon: if a line needs a developer to read it, it does not ship.
