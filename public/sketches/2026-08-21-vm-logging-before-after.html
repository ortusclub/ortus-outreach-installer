VM logging: before and after

Read live from this machine at 12:5x today, campaign MAEN_ANZ_HOB - CCII+CCIII+CCIV, 13 accounts, 1095 of 1497 sent. The VM does not send events. It writes final row states to Postgres, and the APP reconstructs a log from them in _cloudLeadsToLog(). So the log is not a record of what happened, it is a re-description of what the table looks like now. That is why a working VM and a dead VM produce the same screen. Measured on that campaign right now: monitor_check_started_at = 10:27:47Z, monitor_check_completed_at = null, monitor_task_status = null. A check began 25 minutes ago, never finished, and nothing anywhere says so.

Theme

Before · what the VM log says today
Nine lines for eleven hours of work
One line per lead, derived from its final status. No step ever appears. Nothing is emitted while a lead is in flight, so the newest line can be an hour old on a perfectly healthy run. Errors carry LinkedIn's string and no context.
After · the VM streams what it does
Same eleven hours, every step, timestamped
The engine emits an event per step to a campaign_events table and the app tails it. Columns: time, where, stream, text. Same shape as this Mac, so one campaign that moved sides still reads as one story.
