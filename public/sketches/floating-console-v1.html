Floating console — live campaign status

Three quick variants for a floating console that surfaces "something is happening" whenever a campaign is running. All shown over a stub of the dashboard page so you can judge how invasive each is. Hides itself when nothing is running.
Data shown is from the real /api/campaign/status payload: state, name, mode, currentProfile, currentAction.label, processedToday, totalTargets, throttle, errors, parked, last lines of logs. No invented stats.
Variant A
Corner pill — single-line, always visible (bottom-left)
A small pill anchored to the bottom-left of the viewport (position: fixed), always visible across every section of the app — same persistent anchoring pattern as the launch-options trigger. One line: a green pulse, the campaign name, the mode tag, and processed/total. Click expands to the full console. Footprint is tiny — operator can leave it on while navigating Logs, Settings, Accounts. Disappears entirely when state === 'idle'.

Variant B
Bottom dock bar — full width, video-player style
A persistent strip docked to the bottom of the window. Status on the left, the current action mid-line (truncated), counters and the expand toggle on the right. A 2px gold bar underneath shows progress through processedToday / totalTargets. Shows soft warnings (throttle, parked profiles) inline when they fire. Higher visibility than the pill, but eats ~56px of vertical space.

Variant C
Draggable HUD card — corner panel with live feed
A small draggable card that sticks to a corner of the window. Header is grabbable for repositioning; minimize collapses it to the pill from Variant A. Body shows current profile, current lead, processed/total, and the last 2–3 log lines from campaign.logs. Most info per pixel of the three. Operator can park it in a corner that doesn't fight their work.
