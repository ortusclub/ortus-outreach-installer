B2 — three options

When I wrote the spec I assumed Live Cockpit was 3 rows. It's actually a full panel with SVG ring, dual stats, and a progress bar — and it lives inside Live Status, not above it. So "delete the cockpit and absorb into right pane" is bigger than surgical. Three honest options:

αrecommended
Keep the cockpit. Strip "live" claims from the right pane (back to orientation only). Clarify header-stat labels.
