Where to show "this account is assigned to a person". Some accounts are owned by a specific operator (the SoO Assignee column) — before monthly passover only they should use it, so the picker should flag it (the Connect tiles already do this in blue). Below: 3 placements on the breakdown tile. Pick one.

Assignee names here are illustrative (the Assignee field exists; this is about placement). The channel accent still shows the best channel; assignment is the blue layer on top. Examples: gil.cesista assigned to Argil (all In Use) · tara.singh assigned to Dafina (LN OP free) · michael.lontoc pool / unassigned (all free).

V1 · OWNER PILL (right of the email)

A small blue "owner" pill on the email row. Lightest touch; channels read exactly as now.

V2 · ASSIGNED LINE (above the channels)

A blue line between the email and the channel grid, with the passover hint ("frees the 16th"). Most explicit.

V3 · BLUE ACCENT + INLINE OWNER

The left accent turns blue when assigned (overriding the channel colour) and the owner is appended to the email. Strongest "this belongs to someone" signal — but you lose the channel colour on the lip.
