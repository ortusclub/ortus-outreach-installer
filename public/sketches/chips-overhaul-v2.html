The 10 chips
2 active · 2 clean terminal · 2 LinkedIn cap · 3 action · 1 parked
Each chip is its own state with its own label. No more "Done" with seven different sub-meanings or "Warning" hiding three different problems. Visual weight tells you the urgency: white = active · dim dashed = clean terminal · white hairline = LinkedIn capped you · amber = needs you · red = parked.

Batch done · 50/50 Clean terminal. Account finished its campaign target for this run. Resumes next run.
Status sweep done Clean terminal. Bulk Connection-Status check finished. No action needed.
LinkedIn cap · invites LinkedIn limit. Hit weekly invite cap (~200/wk; lower on some accounts). Resumes when LinkedIn lifts it.
LinkedIn cap · InMail LinkedIn limit. InMail credits exhausted. Resumes when LinkedIn refills credits.
Needs login Action required. Session expired — open the GoLogin browser and sign back in.
Rate limited Action required. LinkedIn served a rate-limit page. Open browser to verify, may resume on its own.
Captcha Action required. LinkedIn put up a captcha challenge. Solve it in the browser, then resume.
Parked · too many skips Out of rotation. Auto-pulled mid-run (too many consecutive skips). Press "Try again" to put back.

Visual rules
The chip design follows Bugatti rules: monochrome, hairlines, 9999 radius. The amber and red accents are reserved exclusively for chips that signal action / removal — these are the only places color appears outside the Start CTA gold.
Sending · 18/50 Solid white border = active and going. Pulls focus. Queued Gray text + hairline = passive, waiting. Batch done · 50/50 Dim text + dashed hairline = clean terminal. Recedes — nothing to do. LinkedIn cap · invites White text + hairline = informational fact, slightly louder than dim batch-done. Needs login Amber = the only color reserved for "you need to do something." If you see amber, look at that row first. Parked · too many skips Red = removed from rotation; usually fixable with a "Try again" button.
