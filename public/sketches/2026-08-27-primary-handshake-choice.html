VM CC+IC · where does the primary handshake run?

Asked once, at Launch, when the campaign is CC+IC running on the Cloud VM. The point of the panel is the line at the top: choosing the VM does not mean you can keep the laptop shut. I have written it that way rather than as a neutral two-way choice, because the second option has a known failure mode and pretending otherwise is how people pick it.

The ask

Launch · Connect + Introduce Back on the Cloud VM

Connecting your senders to the primary

Before any introduction can be sent, each sending account has to be a 1st-degree connection of your primary person. Two things have to happen: the senders request the primary, and the primary accepts.

This part is not a choice
The accept always happens on this Mac. Your primary's browser — your local browser, or the GoLogin profile you picked as the primary — has to open here to accept those invitations. Whichever option you choose below, this Mac opens a browser during launch.

Why it is written this way

From the source, not invented

src/cloud-preflight-handshake.js opens with the reason this module exists: “a cloud CC+IC campaign with a LOCAL-ONLY primary never gets its senders connected to the primary. The engine's only sender→primary connect is lazy (fires per-lead at intro time, gated on a lead having been accepted), so a campaign that is monitoring with 0 accepted leads sends the primary NOTHING.”

That is the whole content of the right-hand option's warning. Path A (do it here, before launch) was built specifically to fix it, and today it is the only path that actually runs — Path B stays dormant until the engine ships awaiting_primary_accept.

The “accept always happens on this Mac” line is also literal: the module accepts via launchLocalBrowser or launchProfile, and both are local launchers. Picking a GoLogin profile as the primary changes which browser opens here, never where.
