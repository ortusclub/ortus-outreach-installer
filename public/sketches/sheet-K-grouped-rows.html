How this works in Sheets: use Sheets' native row grouping (Data → Group rows). The bot writes a hidden Group Key column with the stage name, then a sort + group operation creates the collapsible buckets. Group headers are merged cells with the count formula =COUNTIF(GroupKey, "Replied").
Why it's nice: always see the funnel shape (counts per stage). One click drops you into a stage. Replied and DM Now stay open; the 600 long-tail rows hide.
Trade-off: Sheets row grouping has quirks — the +/- toggles live in a thin gutter on the LEFT (like the gray gutter in this sketch) and don't always look this clean.
