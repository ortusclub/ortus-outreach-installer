05 / Connections to the primary · the VM option does nothing

Today

Connect your senders to the primary now?

The second option writes primaryHandshakeOn: 'vm' and the app then skips the handshake. The engine has no code that reads that value — no handshake runs anywhere, no local browser opens, and the senders are never connected to the primary. It is also why no pill can ever show Primary ✓: that flag is only ever written by the local handshake.

A · remove it — my recommendation

Connecting your senders to the primary

Two steps on this Mac: your 3 senders invite the primary, the primary accepts. Then the campaign moves to the cloud. (Shipped wording, v3.1.48.76.)

No choice offered, because there is only one that works. One fewer decision at launch.

B · keep it, honestly labelled

Connect your senders to the primary now?

Either way · the card says where each sender stands

The Primary ✓ suffix already exists in the pill code. What is missing is its opposite: a sender that was never connected currently looks identical to one that was.
