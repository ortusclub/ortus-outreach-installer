The gid is the tab's permanent internal ID (the #gid= in the URL). The app needs it to pin the exact tab; the operator rarely needs to read it. These three options differ in how much of that to surface. Same 4 real tabs from the HTECH workbook in each.

A — Minimal recommended

Tab name + row count only. No gid. System tabs greyed + flagged. Cleanest; fits a native dropdown. The gid is still used under the hood — just not shown.
