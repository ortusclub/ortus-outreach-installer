"Left paused 48h and it stops" — where does the operator hear it?

Three ways to say the same sentence at the moment Pause is pressed. Real components: the toast is .campaign-toast and the dialog is the app's own .modal-backdrop / .modal-card, both from the shipping stylesheet. A is what is live right now (v3.1.28) — B and C are the alternatives.

A · Toast — shipped, no extra click

Pausing — sending stops after the current lead. Left paused for 48h it stops for good and releases its accounts.

Costs nothing and blocks nothing. The risk: it is gone in 7 seconds, and an operator who pauses while looking at another window never sees it. The card keeps the countdown afterwards, and the same sentence is written to the campaign's event log.

B · Dialog — blocks until acknowledged

Pause this campaign?

Sending stops after the lead in flight. The accounts stay reserved for this campaign while it is paused.

If it is still paused in 48 hours it stops for good — the accounts are released and the unsent leads stay queued for a restart.

Impossible to miss the first time. The risk: pause is frequent and instantly reversible, so by the third press the dialog is muscle memory and nobody reads it — and it costs a click every single time.

C · Dialog, but only the first time per campaign

FIRST PAUSE

Pause this campaign?

Sending stops after the lead in flight. If it is still paused in 48 hours it stops for good and releases its accounts — the unsent leads stay queued.

You will not be asked again for this campaign; the card shows the countdown.

Read once, then out of the way — later pauses fall back to A's toast. Costs one extra click per campaign and one flag to remember which campaigns have been warned.
