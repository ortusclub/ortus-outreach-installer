FIX 1 — MONITORING PROMOTED

Monitoring campaigns get their own top-level section between Active and Queued — same visual weight as Active. The "next check in Nm" lives in the section header so you see the rhythm without opening a card.

FIX 2 — SELECT ALWAYS ON

Tiny checkbox column on every row, every section. No "Manage" toggle to find. Selection persists across sections — pick two monitoring + three past, hit Delete, done.

TRADE-OFF

Closest to today's mental model — minimum re-learning. Density similar. Risk: still scrolly when there are many drafts/past. Search/collapse stays in Past as today.
