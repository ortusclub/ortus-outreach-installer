How this works in Sheets: the Inbox tab is a Data → Filter views saved filter on the main pipeline. Filter rule: Next action ∈ {Read & follow up, Retry DM, Verify manually, Wait or withdraw}. Section rows are achieved by sorting on a hidden "Bucket" column.
The badge count is a custom cell with =COUNTIFS(Pipeline!H:H, "Read & follow up") + COUNTIFS(Pipeline!H:H, "Retry DM") + ….
Why it's nice: the operator only opens the Inbox tab. They never see the 600 rows that don't need them. When everything's clean, the tab is empty.
