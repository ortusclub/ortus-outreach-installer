α Throughput Section, Under Accounts most direct interpretation of your suggestion

Approach: immediately below the Accounts section, a Throughput panel. Big gold number = total leads/hour across all selected accounts. Right side shows the math (X accounts × Y/hour = Z). Two knobs (per-account rate, pause range) on the second row. As you check/uncheck accounts, the big number recalculates live.

Why it works: answers "how many will this send per hour" the moment you've finished picking accounts. The math is shown explicitly — no hidden formulas. Daily ceiling is also surfaced in the footer so you don't have to scroll to the daily-limit section to find it.

β Throughput-First one big knob — set total, system divides
