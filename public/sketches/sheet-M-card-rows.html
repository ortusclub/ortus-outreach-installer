How this works in Sheets: set the row height to ~50px and use Format → Wrapping → Wrap on the Lead column. Bot writes the cell with embedded line breaks (CHAR(10)): ="Edwin Silva" & CHAR(10) & "Director · Bayshore" & CHAR(10) & "Replied 1d ago".
The stage pill is its own column to its left (1 word per cell, conditional formatting paints it), or done with rich-text formatting on the same cell (SpreadsheetApp Range.setRichTextValue).
Why it's nice: the lead's name + headline + status all live in one visual block. Less left-right scanning. Looks more modern than a flat grid.
Trade-off: Sheets is awkward at multiline cells — copy/paste, sorting, and filtering all behave a bit differently. Best for read-mostly views.
