Sales Nav Scrape — queue on the dashboard

Six alternatives, built with the app's real cards & CSS. Top three: where the scrape lives on the dashboard. Bottom three: making "where am I in the queue" legible. Pick one from each.

Data honesty: the scrape engine returns only your position, a count of jobs ahead (accountsAhead), and an ETA. It does not send the names of who's ahead. So the "who's above me" alts below show anonymized slots. A named ladder (Steven, Maria…) needs an engine change — flagged at the bottom.
