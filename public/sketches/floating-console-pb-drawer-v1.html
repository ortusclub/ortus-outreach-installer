Floating console — PB-Inspired right-rail drawer

Inspired by Sketch 007 (PB-Inspired). Replaces the bottom-left pill+expand with a right-rail drawer that has filter-chip tabs (Now · Log · Up next), a campaign card with progress + mini Pause/Stop controls, and a richer timestamped log feed. Three variants below — differ only in how the drawer is triggered.
Data shown uses real fields from /api/campaign/status: name, mode, currentAction.{label,lead}, currentProfile, processedToday/totalTargets, logs.slice(-N), errors, parked, throttle. "Up next" reads from scheduled / queued campaigns on the dashboard (real data, not invented). Per project constraint, Ortus runs ONE campaign at a time — the card section never shows multiple.
Variant A · Recommended
Right-edge tab → drawer slides in
Always-visible vertical tab on the right edge of the viewport (when a campaign is running). Click → drawer slides in from the right at 360px wide. Chip-row at top: Now · Log · Up next. The tab handle and drawer never block the main content (right-side reading rail is uncommon in this app). Hides entirely when no campaign is running.
Cost · ~350 LOC over existing console (~120 LOC removed)
