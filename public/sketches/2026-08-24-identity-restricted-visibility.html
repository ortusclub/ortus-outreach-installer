The key distinction: the account is blocked, not necessarily the campaign. These sketches preserve that distinction everywhere the operator can encounter it.

Recommended system

Use all three views as one behavior: disabled red “Blocked” tile before launch, safe-removal banner plus stopped account column during a run, and a compact account-health exception on the dashboard. The campaign itself only turns red when every selected account is blocked and no work can continue.
