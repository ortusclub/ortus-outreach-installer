⚙
Rotation pacing. Per campaign.js, each account has a 6-min turn floor between its own sends, and the worker pool processes ≤5 leads per outer batch. With 5 accounts, one connect roughly every 70-90 seconds wall-clock across the campaign. ~120-160 connects total over 4 h sends (depends on lead count and weekly throttles).
📡
Idle bulk-check (gold marks). shouldFireIdleBulkCheck in campaign.js:145 runs for every parked CC+IC profile. Gates: profile is parked, semaphore has a slot, and ≥5 min since that profile's last bulk-check. When it fires, the profile is briefly relaunched, bulkCheckConnections sweeps the recent connections list, and runAutoIntros fires the IC DM for any lead that just flipped Connected. Each account gets ~8-12 bulk-checks over 4 h, so acceptances are caught mid-campaign without waiting for monitoring.
📝
Sheet writebacks per send. Connection Request Status → "Sent" immediately. If the idle bulk-check sees acceptance, Connection Accepted Status → "Connected" at detection (suppressAcceptedStamp:false, all three call sites). Then the IC DM fires, Introduction Status → "Introduction Made" at success. Sheet shows live progress.
⚠
Edge case to watch. If LinkedIn rate-limits an account (HTTP 429), that account parks, others keep going. If all 5 get rate-limited simultaneously, the campaign stalls but doesn't crash — operator can stop or wait it out.

⏰
Drift behaviour you should expect. If a cycle's 5-account sweep takes 7 min, the next cycle fires at T+15 (8 min later, since prev-boundary-based). If a sweep takes 20 min (longer than the 15-min cadence — rare but possible if LinkedIn throttles), the catch-up formula in 5d55ba7 jumps to the next 15-min boundary strictly after now (T+30, not T+45 — that off-by-one was the just-shipped fix).
🎯
Where the IC DMs land. Each account checks its OWN recent connections only. Lead-to- account routing is handled by runAutoIntros's rowByUrl lookup: the lead's row contains the account that originally sent the connect, and the bulk-check filters to that account's invites. So if "kenya5 invited X", only kenya5 will fire the IC DM to X — even if the monitoring sweep visits all 5 profiles.
⚠
Throughput sanity check. 5 accounts × ~1-2 min each = 5-10 min per cycle. 96 cycles/day × 5-10 min = 480-960 min/day of bulk-checking = 8-16 h/day. That sounds high, but each account is parked between checks — the laptop is mostly idle. If you set cadence to 15 min, expect the GoLogin browsers to open/close ~96 times per day per account. Verify your laptop's RAM doesn't balloon.
