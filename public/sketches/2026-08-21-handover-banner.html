E · the handover banner

The moment a run moves between this Mac and the Cloud VM is the moment the operator is most likely to think the app has hung and start clicking. Today that moment is announced in 12.5px grey text that hides itself after five seconds, on one surface only. Drive it below and watch both behave.

Start a handover Then idle

Nothing above is part of the design. It stands in for the events the app already emits, so the behaviour can be watched rather than described. Start a handover, then wait past five seconds without pressing "Handover finishes": that is the whole bug, and the two panels below diverge exactly there.

After · surface 2, the campaign board strip
.sn-strip › .sn-vjcard

The board's card is a clone of the dashboard's card, so building the banner into .vj-card lands it on both surfaces at once. Nothing here is a second implementation, which is why both panels above move together. The strip keeps its own header untouched. The old standalone #sn-handover is deleted, not left running alongside, or the operator gets told twice in two different voices.

Rules this banner follows

1. It stays up until the handover is actually finished. No timeout.
2. It names both ends, every time. Never a bare "Handover".
3. It says what the operator does, and the answer is always: nothing, wait.
4. It is the same component as CHECKING and SENDING, so all three read alike.
5. No em dashes. The three copy branches at app.js:227-235 lose theirs.
6. It is not called .sn-switch. That name is taken by the Log/Counts tab switcher at app.js:9416.
