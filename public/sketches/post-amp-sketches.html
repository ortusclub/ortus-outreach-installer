Post Amplification — UI Sketches

Three variants of the per-account engagement panel · v1 · 2026-05-07

Page-level layout is the same across all 3 (Post URL → Account picker → Engagement panel → Start). Only the engagement panel changes. Pick one and I'll build phase 1.

Wide Table
One row per account · columns: Account · Like · Comment · Comment text · Suggestions. Inline.
Densest. Scans at a glance. Good for 5-15 accounts.
Comment textarea pinches at narrower widths. Suggestions live as a small ▾ link.
