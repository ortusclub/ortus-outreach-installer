<objective>
Fix two coupled bugs introduced during quick-260423-eiw's Open Profile → Sales Nav routing:

1. **Wrong OP gating signal.** `sendViaSalesNav` currently treats "no credit counter" as proof of Open Profile, but the panel can render without a credit counter for non-OP reasons (slow render, A/B variant, paid panel stripped). This produces false positives that send to non-OP leads. LinkedIn shows a positive OP marker — the literal badge text "Free to Open Profile" in the Sales Nav message panel — which we must use instead.

2. **Duplicated /in/ → Sales Nav conversion.** The conversion logic lives in two places (outreach.js's force_open_profile branch and inside sendInMail). The force_inmail /in/ path also has an opportunistic `sendOpenProfileMessage` attempt that false-rejects free senders for the same reason as Bug 1 — which disappears once conversion happens upfront and we delegate to the deterministic `sendViaSalesNav` flow.

Purpose: Eliminate the false-positive OP send risk (Bug 1) AND simplify the OP/InMail routing to one uniform shape — "convert to Sales Nav upfront, then delegate to sendViaSalesNav" — collapsing ~90 lines of near-duplicate logic down to ~35 lines and removing the now-dead `sendOpenProfileMessage` helper (Bug 2).

Output: Two modified files (`src/linkedin/actions.js`, `src/linkedin/outreach.js`), `sendOpenProfileMessage` deleted, test suite still green.
</objective>
